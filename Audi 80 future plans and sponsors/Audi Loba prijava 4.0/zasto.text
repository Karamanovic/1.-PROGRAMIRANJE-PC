The Audi 80 B3 1.8 Turbo (KARAS ENTERPRISE) is a truly unique project — a car that has become a rare sight on the streets of the Balkans. <br> You may still spot one or two on the street racing scene, but the real vision here is to turn this example into a rare and striking OEM+ gem: authentic enough to impress true enthusiasts, yet stylish enough to stand out anywhere.
<p>
The foundation is strong and reliable — a galvanized body resistant to rust, already sourced rare and valuable parts (S-line leather interior, Nardi steering wheel, digital climate control), and an engine with huge potential. There’s no need for extreme wide-body conversions or heavy modifications: with smart servicing, targeted upgrades, and OEM+ styling, this Audi can quickly become one of the most eye-catching and photogenic builds on the scene.
</p>
This is not just “another project” — it’s an opportunity to show how with the right vision and support, a rare classic can be transformed into a story worth remembering.
