Why me?

The Audi 80 B3 1.8 Turbo is a truly unique project — a car that has become a rare sight on the streets of the Balkans.
You may still spot one or two on the street racing scene, but the real vision here is to turn this example into a rare and striking OEM+ gem: authentic enough to impress true enthusiasts, yet stylish enough to stand out anywhere.

The foundation is strong and reliable — a galvanized body resistant to rust, already sourced rare and valuable parts (S-line leather interior, Nardi steering wheel, digital climate control), and an engine with huge potential (300hp+). There’s no need for extreme wide-body conversions or heavy modifications: with smart servicing, targeted upgrades, and OEM+ styling, this Audi can quickly become one of the most eye-catching and photogenic builds on the scene.

This is not just “another car project” — it’s an opportunity to show how with the right vision and support, a rare classic can be transformed into a story worth remembering.

Why the Audi 80 B3 1.8 Turbo is a great sponsor opportunity (for LOBA)

1. Strong foundation — galvanized body The galvanized chassis offers built-in rust protection, reducing hidden costs and cutting time on body repairs.

2. Rare and valuable parts already sourced: Nardi wheel, front coupe bummper, plus a full digital climate unit ready for install.

3. No extreme modifications needed

4. Quick wins & strong content potential smaller jobs (climate install, heated seats, intercooler, wheels) are perfect “before/after” reels.

5. Owner has already allocated €250–300 for base parts — proof of commitment. Sponsor joins a project with momentum, not a dead start.

6. Authentic story & emotional value Owned for 4+ years, plus limited resources — builds a relatable and marketable narrative for LOBA’s channels.

7. Growth & monetization potential Beyond restoration, the project aims to produce custom carbon parts (spoilers, hoods, bumpers). That opens co-branding and revenue opportunities for a sponsor.

8. Fast timeline, clear results with no major bodywork needed, progress can be visible quickly. A 12-month timeline is realistic and appealing for both storytelling and results.
