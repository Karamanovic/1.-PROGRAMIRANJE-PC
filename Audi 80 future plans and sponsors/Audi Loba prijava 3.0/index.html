KARAS ENTERPRISE — Audi 80 B3 1.8 Turbo

Emotivna veza. Profesionalna vizija. Spreman za sponzora.

Priča projekta

Vozilo: Audi 80 B3 1.8 Turbo, oko 300.000 km. Karoserija pocinčana; S-line kožna sjedišta iz A4 B7; Nardi volan; fabrički ventilirajući diskovi; kupljena digitalna klima. Cilj: elegancija + povremeni sound.

Podatci o vozilu

Engine1.8 Turbo (≈300,000 km)
TransmissionStock 5-speed from Audi 100 c4 2.0 benzin (plan: Audi S3 6-speed if possible)
SeatsFront + Rear S-line leather (A4 B7) — heating to be wired
SuspensionStock (plan: KW Gewinde or Air)
WheelsDesired: 17-18" Rotor / BBS, conversion 4x108 → 5x112 via hub
BrakesPlan: 280mm+ discs with Brembo / Porsche calipers (4/6 piston)
OtherDigital climate (purchased), black headliner, Competition spoiler, coupe bumper w/ fogs, carbon fiber hood, carbon fiber interior

Ciljevi projekta

  • Elegantni OEM+ stanced daily driver
  • Poboljšanje hlađenja, turbo i ECU
  • Produkcija sadržaja: reels, foto, eventi

S-line leather seats wired for heating, black headliner, digital climate integration.
